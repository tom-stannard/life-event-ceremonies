## Weddings

### Wedding Ceremonies

Country, city, modern, traditional, formal, casual or cultural theme options can all be incorporated into your wedding ceremony.

You can write your own vows or I can provide you with a selection of written vows to choose from. I can perform unplugged (no PA) if that suits you too. 
I can tell your love story; I can recommend readings or rituals and provide some props and decoration for your ceremony space.

But most importantly I can take care of you and the guests for the ceremony and relieve the stress that you might be feeling on the day.


Your ceremony - your way.



## Vow Renewals

### Vow Renewal Ceremonies
How much fun would it be to give your original wedding day a do over! That's how it feels for many couples who choose to renew their wedding vows.

Maybe you were married in a religion that you no longer practice, in front of friends that you no longer see. Maybe as an older person you want to say and promise things differently to your partner.

Vow renewals have no legal criteria to meet so we can make them as fun, romantic, or serious as you want them to be.

I have loads of resources vows, poems, readings, rituals or maybe write your own. I can't wait to hear your story.


## Baby Naming Ceremonies

### Naming Ceremonies

What better way to introduce a new family member than to host a Naming Ceremony with your loved ones present. As there are no legal obligations with these ceremonies you have full control over how we create a unique and memorable experience.

I have a lot of resources including candles, lanterns, poems, and readings.  We can perform  ceremonial rituals like rose petal blessings, tree planting, bubble wishes and sand blending ceremonies to include the whole family. 

Guide-parents, Godparents or Grandparents can be chosen to play a part in the ceremony and I have a list of heartwarming vows that can be made by friends and family.

These ceremonies are full of fun, laughter and love.

Your ceremony - your way.


## Funerals

### Funeral Ceremonies

There is no doubt that funerals are a sad and stressful time in your life. It is my hope that I can make this difficult time easier for you when working with you to create a funeral ceremony.
​
Funerals can be a time of celebration of a life lived, an acknowledgement of a loved one's achievements and a time of hope, love and support for the family and friends in the days ahead.

I have many resources that we can draw on to create a truly unique and respectful ceremony.

Your ceremony - your way.
