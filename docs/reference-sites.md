# Reference sites

Other celebrant sites the owner likes, used to guide **content structure**. They are not a guide for visual design. Write our own copy in Michelle's voice; don't copy their text.

## cindycheriechilds.au

<https://www.cindycheriechilds.au/>. Surveyed 2026-11-15. The owner pointed to its **ceremony types, location pages, FAQs and testimonials** as the model to follow. Ignore its venues page.

### Ceremony pages

Each ceremony type has its own page with the same template:

- Tagline (small heading) above an H1 for the ceremony type.
- Two calls to action at the top: plan or explore a ceremony, and contact.
- 2–3 intro paragraphs covering the style options and the rituals offered (handfasting, unity candle, sand ceremony, tree planting, time capsule).
- A package block for each offering: name, "From $X to $Y" price range, an "Includes:" bullet list, and an "Enquire now" button.

Their ceremony types: Weddings (packages from legals-only to all-inclusive, plus pre-wedding coaching), Vow Renewals, Baby Naming, Celebrations of Life & Memorials, Other Ceremonies (commitment ceremonies, home blessings, blended family rituals, personal milestones, on custom quotes).

### Location pages

`/locations/<area>` (Greater Sydney, Illawarra Region, Southern Highlands), reached from a "Locations" dropdown in the nav.

- The title is `Celebrant | <Area> | <Name>` and the H1 is `<Area> Wedding Celebrant`. The location name is the main SEO keyword.
- **Their weakness:** the body copy is identical on every location page and doesn't mention the area, the footer doesn't link to them, and one H1 has a typo. Ours should have copy specific to each area (local venues and landmarks, travel, which ceremonies are offered there) and be linked from the footer and the ceremony pages.

### FAQs

One FAQ page. All six questions are about weddings: what's included, what a "legals only" wedding is, travel outside the home area, symbolic rituals, LGBTQIA+ couples, and how far ahead to book. Our FAQs should cover every ceremony type, and could also appear on each ceremony page.

### Testimonials

Shown on the home page, the location pages and a dedicated `/testimonials` page. Each is a quote paragraph with names in "Name & Name" format. There are no photos or star ratings. The testimonials page has a "Leave a review" button linking to Google reviews.

### Other patterns worth reusing

- **About page:** a personal intro, then "why I do this", then "my approach" as bullet points.
- **Enquiry form fields:** name, email, phone, ceremony type, preferred date, ceremony location, "tell me about your vision", how did you hear about me.
- **Calls to action:** "Contact me" and "Enquire now" repeated on every page.
- **Footer:** contact details, social links, ABN, professional memberships (e.g. AFCC, Coalition of Celebrant Associations).
