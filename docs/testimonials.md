## Using them on the site

- Show the reviewer's name as a first name, or a couple's names where known (e.g. "Sam & Emma"), rather than Google usernames such as "Fire and Water" or "rachealwatkins".
- Light tidying of spelling and punctuation is fine; keep the meaning and wording.
- Link to the Google reviews page ("Read more reviews on Google") once we have the profile URL.
- **Themes that come up again and again**, useful when writing the copy: she puts people at ease from the first meeting; warmth and calm; a great sense of humour; stays relaxed with last-minute plans, lateness and big families; handles rain, wind and storms with a smile; includes children naturally; listens and personalises; intimate weddings, elopements and weddings at home.
